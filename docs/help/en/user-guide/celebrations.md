# Praise and celebrations

Adaptive Learner celebrates your progress so that learning feels
rewarding - without getting in your way. Celebrations are
**earned**, never patronizing: the more significant the
achievement, the more prominent the feedback.

## What you'll see

- **Correct answers** get a quick green pulse, a check icon, and
  occasionally a short praise phrase below the answer. A wrong
  answer shows a gentle red flash and the correction (the exact
  words to fix) - never criticism.
- **Finishing a lesson** reveals your score counting up, your
  star rating, and an encouraging message. A perfect (3-star)
  run adds a confetti burst.
- **Milestones** - a 7/30/100-day streak, a new level, 50/100/500
  mastered elements, or a new badge - appear as a brief banner at
  the top of the screen and dismiss themselves.

## Feedback intensity

**Settings > Interface > Feedback Intensity** lets you choose how
loud the celebrations are:

- **Subtle** - only the correct/wrong colour. No phrases, no
  confetti, no milestone banners.
- **Normal** (default) - animations, praise phrases, and confetti
  on a perfect score.
- **Enthusiastic** - everything, plus milestone banners and a
  praise phrase on every correct answer.

If your device requests reduced motion, animations are switched
off automatically and the intensity is kept subtle, regardless of
this setting.

## Sounds

Sounds are **off by default** - you are never surprised by audio.
Turn them on in **Settings > Interface > Sounds**, then use the
volume slider and the **Test** button to preview. Sounds are
short synthesized chimes and are always supplementary: every cue
they give is also shown on screen.
