# Daily missions

Daily missions give you a few concrete, achievable goals to pursue
each day - a reason to open the app and a gentle nudge toward a
learning habit. They are **optional**: the app works exactly the
same without them, and there is **no penalty** for missing a day.
Tomorrow simply brings a fresh set.

## How they work

- You get up to **3 missions** per day, shown on the Dashboard
  between your streak and your badges.
- Missions are **achievable** for anyone who uses the app 15-30
  minutes, and the difficulty **adapts** to you: a new learner
  gets gentle "learning" + "exploration" goals; once you have
  some error history, "review" and "mastery" missions join in.
- They are **deterministic**: the same day shows you the same
  missions across your devices, and the same mission is never
  given two days in a row.
- Progress updates automatically from what you already do -
  finishing lessons, reviewing elements, mastering words. When a
  mission completes you earn its bonus **XP**.
- At local midnight, unfinished missions quietly expire and new
  ones are assigned.

## Configuring missions

**Settings > Learning > Daily Missions** lets you:

- turn daily missions **on or off** (on by default),
- choose **how many** per day (1-3),
- pick a **difficulty mix** - Balanced (default), Easy, or
  Challenging,
- **reset** today's missions to get a fresh set.

## Streak joker

If you keep a mission streak going and then miss a single day, a
streak freeze (earned over time) can bridge that one gap so your
streak survives. A second missed day ends it - but, again, there
is never a penalty, just a fresh start.
