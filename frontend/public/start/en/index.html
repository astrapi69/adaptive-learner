An app that adapts to you, not the other way around.

Create your own learning material, with AI.

Most learning apps hand you a finished course to follow. Adaptive Learner turns that around: you decide what you learn, and the app adapts - to your goal, your pace, your mistakes.

Open the app in your browser Download the launcher

Your material instead of a fixed course

Generate learning content with AI from whatever occupies you: a book, a text, a question. Or start with ready-made learning sets and build on them. What you get wrong comes back at the right time, until it sticks.

Free, open, yours

Adaptive Learner is free and open source (MIT license). The app runs on your own machine: no sign-up, no account, your learning data stays with you. Creating content with AI uses your own key with an AI provider; learning with ready-made sets needs none.

What there is to learn

Language courses in several languages and subject topics, from software to psychology - plus everything you make from them yourself. The content lives openly in public repositories.

How to start

  1. Download the desktop launcher for Linux, macOS or Windows from the release page.
  2. Run it. It needs Docker, checks that itself and helps you set it up.
  3. The launcher starts the app and opens it in your browser.

Without installing anything: the browser version runs right away, with your data stored in the browser.
