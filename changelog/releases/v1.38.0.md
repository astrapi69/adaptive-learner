# Adaptive Learner v1.38.0

**Released**: 2026-05-29

Phase 55 — **EXP-008 Lob und Celebration (the emotional layer)**.
Everything mechanical already worked - errors are tracked,
adaptive lessons are generated, XP and badges are awarded - but
the *moment of success* felt flat. v1.38.0 makes learning feel
rewarding with satisfying, earned micro-feedback: no obnoxious
popups, scaled to the significance of the achievement, off-by-
default sound, and a full `prefers-reduced-motion` path.

## What's new

### Praise phrases (55A)

- New `backend/config/praise/{8 langs}.yaml` catalogs across five
  event categories (correct answer, lesson complete, streak
  milestone, mastery, improvement), DE + EN handwritten and the
  other six AI-translated (pending native review). Parallel key
  sets so phrases can be tracked by key.
- `make sync-praise` bundles them to `frontend/src/data/praise/`.
- `phrase-picker.ts`: no-repeat session cycling - the whole pool
  is shown before any phrase repeats - with EN fallback.

### Answer micro-animations (55B)

- A shared `AnswerCelebration` across all five exercise types:
  haptic pulse + a short praise phrase on a correct answer (gated
  by intensity + frequency). CSS keyframes give a green pulse on
  correct, a gentle red flash on wrong, an icon slide-in, and a
  praise fade. Wrong answers never carry a negative phrase - the
  token-diff carries the correction.

### Celebration screen (55C)

- The lesson summary counts the score up from 0, shows a per-star
  encouraging message, and on a perfect (3-star) run adds a
  dynamic praise phrase and a CSS-only confetti burst (30
  GPU-accelerated particles, no canvas, no library, zero bundle
  weight, self-dismissing).

### Milestone overlays (55D)

- Threshold detection for streaks (7/30/100 days), mastery
  (50/100/500 elements) and any level-up, a de-duplicating
  celebration queue, and a top-anchored auto-dismissing overlay.
  Multiple milestones show sequentially.

### Feedback intensity (55E)

- Settings > Interface gains a three-level control: **Subtle**
  (colour only), **Normal** (default - animations + phrases +
  confetti on a perfect score), **Enthusiastic** (everything +
  milestone overlays + praise on every correct answer). Changes
  take effect live, no reload.

### Sounds (55F)

- Six synthesized sound effects (correct/wrong/star/confetti/
  badge/level-up) rendered at runtime into AudioBuffers from
  additive recipes - zero audio files. **OFF by default**; a
  toggle, volume slider and Test button live in Settings. The
  AudioContext is created lazily on first play (autoplay-safe).

### Event bus + stats wiring (55G, 55H)

- `celebration-bus.ts` is the decoupled dispatch: emit plays the
  mapped sound and notifies subscribers; `celebrate*` helpers
  route milestones through the queue. Lesson completion snapshots
  gamification before/after and celebrates every milestone and
  newly-earned badge. "Best streak" is the existing maintained
  `longest_streak_days`, now highlighted when the current streak
  is at the all-time best.

## Accessibility + safety

- `prefers-reduced-motion` suppresses **all** animations and
  forces the effective intensity to subtle (no confetti, no
  overlays); state transitions become instant.
- Sounds are always supplementary - every cue is also shown.
- The celebration layer is defensive: a failure never breaks the
  lesson flow.
